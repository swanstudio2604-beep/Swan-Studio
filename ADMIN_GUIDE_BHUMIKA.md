# 🌸 Swan Studio — Bhumika's Quick Phone Guide
**The complete, easy-to-use manual for managing your handcrafted crochet shop from your phone.**

---

## 📱 How to Open Your Store Admin
1. Open Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) on your phone.
2. Go to: **`https://swan-studio.onslate.in/admin`**
   *(If you're not logged in, it will automatically take you to the secure login page).*
3. Type your administrative email and password, then tap **Sign In as Atelier Owner**.
4. 💡 **Phone Tip — Add to Home Screen:**
   - **On iPhone (Safari):** Tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom &rarr; tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   - **On Android (Chrome):** Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner &rarr; tap **"Add to Home screen"**.
   - Now you have a Swan Studio Atelier icon right on your phone screen just like an app!

> [!TIP]
> **Fast Direct Database Connection:** Swan Studio on `swan-studio.onslate.in` connects directly to PostgreSQL on Supabase with zero server cold starts or sleep delays!

---

## 🧭 Your Mobile Navigation (Bottom Bar)
At the bottom of your phone screen, you will always see 5 quick buttons:
- 📊 **Overview:** Today's & this week's sales, pending custom orders, and low-stock alerts.
- 🛒 **Orders:** All customer orders, shipping labels, and one-tap WhatsApp chat buttons.
- 🧶 **Products:** Add new crochet creations, change prices, and upload camera photos.
- ✨ **Custom:** View bespoke inquiries from customers with their color palettes and photos.
- ☰ **More:** Opens the full menu for Collections, Inventory, UPI Payments, Coupons, Reviews, Customers, Website Content, and Store Settings.

## 🛍️ Daily Tasks You Can Do in 30 Seconds

### 1. Adding a New Crochet Creation (with Phone Camera)
1. Tap **🧶 Products** in the bottom bar &rarr; tap **➕ Add New Creation**.
2. Type the name (e.g. *Pastel Daisy Bouquet*), pick its collection, and set the price in ₹.
3. Tap **📷 Take or Select Photos**:
   - You can take pictures directly with your phone camera or pick from your gallery.
   - **Automatic Compression:** Phone photos are often 5–10 MB. Swan Studio automatically shrinks them down to under 350 KB so your pages load lightning-fast across India without eating up your mobile data!
   - Tap any photo to set it as the **Primary (Cover) Photo**.
4. Set how many units you have ready in stock (e.g. `3`).
5. Tap **💾 Save Creation**. It appears immediately on your public shop!

### 2. Quick Stock Adjustments (+ / -)
- On the **Overview** dashboard, any item with 2 or fewer pieces will appear in the **⚠️ Low Stock Quick Restock** box.
- Tap **`+`** to add 1 piece when you finish stitching one, or tap **`-`** when sold at an in-person pop-up.

### 3. Fulfilling an Order & Chatting with Customers
1. Tap **🛒 Orders** in the bottom bar.
2. Filter by status (e.g., *New*, *Confirmed*, *In Progress*).
3. Under each customer order card, you have two instant buttons:
   - 💬 **WhatsApp:** Opens WhatsApp with a prefilled greeting including their Order ID and status!
   - 📞 **Call:** Instantly dials the customer's phone number.
4. When you ship a package via India Post or courier:
   - Tap **Update Status** &rarr; select **Dispatched**.
   - Paste the **Tracking Number** and courier name.
   - Tap **Save Changes**.

### 4. Responding to Custom Orders (Bespoke Requests)
1. Tap **✨ Custom** in the bottom bar.
2. Review the customer's idea, occasion, preferred color palette, and reference photo.
3. Tap **Update Stage**:
   - Enter your quotation price (e.g. `₹1,800`) and required deposit (e.g. `₹500`).
   - Tap the 💬 **WhatsApp** button to immediately send the quotation to the customer.
   - Once they pay the deposit, move the status to **In Progress (Making)**.

### 5. Moderating Customer Reviews
1. Tap **☰ More** &rarr; tap **Customer Reviews**.
2. Real customer reviews show up here. You can tap **✓ Approve** so it shows on the public site, or **Hide** / **Delete**.
3. *(To protect your brand integrity, fake reviews cannot be fabricated from the admin).*

### 6. Changing Website Text & Photos (Without Code!)
1. Tap **☰ More** &rarr; tap **Website Content & Story**.
2. You can edit:
   - The top announcement banner text.
   - The homepage hero headline and picture.
   - Your artisan story ("Meet Bhumika") and portrait photo.
   - Your shipping and return policies.
3. Tap **💾 Save All Changes**. The public website updates instantly!

### 7. Exporting Orders to Excel / CSV
- Go to **🛒 Orders** and tap **📥 Export CSV**. A spreadsheet file with customer names, phones, shipping addresses, items, and payments downloads directly to your phone.

## 🔒 Security Reminders for Bhumika
- **Keep Your Password Safe:** Never share your admin password on public groups or social media.
- **Lockout Protection:** If someone tries typing the wrong password 5 times in a row, the admin locks them out for 15 minutes.
- **Logout:** When using a shared computer or tablet, tap **☰ More** &rarr; **Log Out** to end your session.
