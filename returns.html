Returns, Exchanges & Care

Artisanal guarantee & care guidelines from our Jaipur atelier

✨ Latest Returns Notice: Due to the artisanal, custom nature of handcrafted crochet, each piece is crafted with patient care and inspected before dispatch. For transit damage, reach out on WhatsApp within 48 hours for immediate replacement.

1. Artisanal & Handmade Policy

Because every Swan Studio creation is individually handmade with significant time and bespoke yarn preparation, we do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind or personal preference once delivered.

2. Damaged or Defective Items

We take immense pride in quality inspection. If your package arrives damaged due to postal handling:

  • Please record a short unboxing video or take clear photographs upon delivery.
  • Send the photos/video to Bhumika via WhatsApp (+91 9509966512) within 48 hours of receiving the parcel.
  • We will review the issue immediately and provide either a free repair, replacement piece, or refund.

3. Non-Returnable Items

  • Custom-commissioned pieces (custom initials, bespoke color schemes, personalized plushies).
  • Items damaged due to improper customer care (e.g. machine washing, bleaching, rough handling).

4. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed manually by the administrator directly back to your original UPI ID within 2–4 business days. The studio's database logs all refund status changes with an audit record.

5. Contact for Support

For any questions about your order or return eligibility, reach out directly on WhatsApp at +91 9509966512.
