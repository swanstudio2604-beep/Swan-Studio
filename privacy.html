Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

⚖️
Draft Notice: This policy template is a starter document to be reviewed, approved, or modified by the studio owner (Bhumika Nama). It does not constitute formal legal counsel.

1. Information We Collect

At Swan Studio, we collect only the necessary information required to craft, process, and deliver your handmade crochet orders. This includes:

  • Your name and contact details (WhatsApp number, email address)
  • Delivery address and postal code
  • Custom design specifications and reference notes
  • UPI transaction IDs or reference numbers submitted for manual payment verification

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Fulfill orders and communicate regarding custom crochet timelines
  • Verify manual UPI payments directly against our bank records
  • Dispatch parcel tracking details via WhatsApp or SMS
  • Provide customer assistance and support

3. Data Protection & Security

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third-party advertisers. All customer data and authentication records are securely guarded with Row Level Security (RLS) policies in our database. Customers can only access their own order records.

4. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have questions about your personal data or wish to request data updates, please contact Bhumika Nama directly via WhatsApp at +91 9509966512.
