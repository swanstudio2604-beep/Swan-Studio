Terms & Conditions

Store terms and customer agreement for Swan Studio

1. Handmade Nature & Subtle Variations

Every item offered at Swan Studio is 100% handmade by Bhumika Nama using high-grade yarns and fibers. Because human hands create each stitch, minor variations in dimension, color hue (due to yarn dye lots), or stitch tension are natural signatures of artisanal crochet and not product defects.

2. Manual UPI Payment Policy

Swan Studio operates without an automated payment gateway to minimize customer fees.

  • All payments are transferred manually via UPI to 9509966512@fam.
  • After payment, customers must submit their UPI Transaction/UTR Reference ID.
  • Orders are never confirmed automatically. An order transitions from Pending Verification to Paid / Confirmed only after Bhumika manually verifies receipt in the studio's bank account.

3. Custom Orders & Inquiries

Submitting a custom order request through the website is an inquiry. A custom commission is confirmed only after scope, pricing, color palette, and completion dates are mutually agreed upon, and initial deposit or full payment is verified.

4. Order Cancellation Window

Cancellations for standard catalog items can be requested within 12 hours of order placement. For custom or personalized items, cancellations are only possible prior to the commencement of crafting work.

5. Contact Information

For queries regarding these terms, reach out via WhatsApp at +91 9509966512.
