Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: October 2026

📦
Draft Notice: This shipping policy template outlines operational guidelines for handmade dispatch across India, subject to final rates and courier selection by Bhumika Nama.

1. Crafting & Preparation Times

Because our products are crafted by hand, dispatch timelines depend on availability:

  • Ready to Ship: Dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment verification.
  • Made to Order: Typically requires 3–6 business days to knit, shape, and package with care.
  • Complex Custom Commissions: Timeline communicated during custom quotation.

2. Delivery Across India

We deliver pan-India via reputable domestic courier partners (e.g. DTDC, Delhivery, India Post Speed Post). Once dispatched, delivery generally takes:

  • Metro Cities: 3–5 business days
  • Other Cities & Towns: 4–7 business days

3. Order Tracking

As soon as your package is handed over to the courier service, Bhumika shares your consignment tracking number and courier tracking link directly with you via WhatsApp.

4. Protective Packaging

We take extreme care in packaging your crochet treasures. Bouquets are cushioned with kraft tissue paper and shipped in rigid boxes to ensure flowers, petals, and stems arrive in pristine, uncrushed condition.

5. Urgent or Date-Sensitive Orders

If you need a bouquet for a specific birthday, anniversary, or celebration date, please inform us on WhatsApp prior to ordering so we can confirm feasibility.
